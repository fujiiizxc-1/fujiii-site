01 / The house

A private edge of the blue.

Fujii Hotel sits lightly above the sea, made from local stone, salt air, and the belief that a beautiful stay should leave room for your own rhythm.

Mornings begin with light across the water. Afternoons disappear beside the pool. By evening, the house turns toward the horizon and lets the day soften around you.
